About Goobrow

Goobrow is a search platform designed to make finding information on the web simple, fast, and accessible.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a clean search experience that brings useful information together through one simple interface.

Search Technology

Goobrow can use multiple search sources and services to help provide relevant results. Results are presented through the Goobrow interface.

Built for the Web and Android

Goobrow is designed to work through its website and Android application, providing a consistent search experience across devices.

Our Commitment

We aim to continuously improve Goobrow's speed, usability, reliability, and search experience.
